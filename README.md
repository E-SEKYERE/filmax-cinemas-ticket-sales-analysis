# 🎬 Filmax Cinemas — Q3 Ticket Sales Analysis & Excel Dashboard

![Project Overview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b7ffda0-9390-480d-8f90-cf7d284c35fa)


## 📌 Project Summary
Filmax Cinemas is a movie theatre chain operating across California, New York, Texas, and Florida, offering Regular, VIP, Student, Couple, and Family ticket categories. Despite strong weekend attendance, management lacked visibility into which movies, branches, and showtimes were actually driving revenue. This project analyses **2,000 Q3 ticket transactions** (July–September) using Excel PivotTables and builds a **dynamic, month-filterable dashboard** (with light and dark mode views) to support scheduling, pricing, and marketing decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel** — PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and dashboard design
- **Excel formulas** — MoM (month-on-month) growth tracking, dynamic KPI cards

## ✨ Key Features
- KPI cards for Revenue, Orders, and Tickets Sold, with month-on-month % change
- Month slicer (Jul / Aug / Sep) to filter the entire dashboard dynamically
- Light mode and dark mode dashboard views
- Weekly trend chart identifying the highest-revenue days of the week
- Movie-level revenue ranking across all 8 films screened in Q3
- Time-of-day donut chart (Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Late Night) showing when tickets are sold
- Ticket category revenue contribution breakdown
- Location-based analysis tracking revenue by U.S. state

## 📂 Data Source
A Q3 ticket sales dataset of 2,000 transactions covering Sales ID, Movie Name, Show Date, Month, Weekday, Show Time, Ticket Category, Tickets Sold, Ticket Price, Payment Method, Cinema Branch, and Total Revenue. Data has been anonymised for demonstration.

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Total Q3 performance:** $95,765 in revenue from 2,000 orders and 6,026 tickets sold
- **Top movie by revenue:** *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 4* ($17,581), ahead of *Barbie* ($13,461) and *Mission Impossible 8* ($12,015); *Fast X* was the lowest earner ($9,361)
- **Best-performing branch:** New York generated $34,613 — nearly 1.5x California, the next-highest state ($22,537)
- **Weekend demand dominates:** Saturday ($23,645) and Sunday ($22,123) together account for roughly 48% of Q3 revenue, far outpacing weekday takings
- **Evening is peak showtime:** 978 of 2,000 transactions (49%) were for evening screenings, more than the other three time slots combined
- **Regular tickets drive revenue:** Regular tickets contributed 38% of total revenue, followed by VIP (23%); Student tickets, while cheapest, contributed the least (8%)
- **Month-on-month growth:** August was the strongest month ($34,915, +16% MoM), with orders and ticket volume both climbing over 12% before easing slightly in September
